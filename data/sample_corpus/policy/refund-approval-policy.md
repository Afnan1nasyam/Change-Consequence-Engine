# Refund Approval Policy

Owner: Payments Governance
Status: Approved
Applies to: all regions unless a regional policy states otherwise

## Purpose

This policy sets the authorisation required before a customer refund is released. It exists
so that refunds above a material amount receive a second pair of eyes, and so that the
finance close is not disrupted by unreviewed credits.

## Approval limits

Refunds of 10,000 USD or more require manager approval before release. A refund below that
amount may be released by any agent in the Payments Support team without a second approval.

Approval must be recorded in the case record. A verbal approval is not sufficient.

## Delegation

A manager may delegate approval authority to a named deputy for a period of no more than
14 days. The delegation must be recorded in the access management system.

## Exceptions

A refund arising from a confirmed billing defect may be released without manager approval
regardless of amount, provided the defect has an open incident record. This exception exists
because withholding a refund for a fault we caused compounds the original harm.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by Payments Governance.
