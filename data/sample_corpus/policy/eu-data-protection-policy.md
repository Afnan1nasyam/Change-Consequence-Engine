# EU Data Protection Policy

Owner: EU Data Protection Officer
Status: Approved
Scope: region:EU

This policy implements regional obligations for the EU. It narrows the global Data Retention
Policy where regional law requires a shorter period.

## Retention period

Personal data is retained for 30 days after the account is closed, then deleted. This is
shorter than the global period and governs for the EU.

## Lawful basis records

The lawful basis for each processing activity is recorded in the processing register and
reviewed when the activity changes.

## Data subject requests

A data subject request is acknowledged within 72 hours and completed within one month of
receipt.
