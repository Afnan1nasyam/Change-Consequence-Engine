# Refund Handling — New Agent Onboarding

Slide deck, Payments Support onboarding. Revision 7.

## Slide 4 — When do I need approval?

Most refunds you handle will not need a manager. Refunds of 10,000 USD or more require
manager approval before you release them. Anything below that, you can action yourself once
you have confirmed the customer's identity.

## Slide 5 — How to request approval

Use the case record. Tag your team manager and select "Refund approval" as the request type.
Do not release the refund until the approval appears in the case history.

## Slide 6 — Common mistakes

- Releasing a large refund before the approval lands. This is the most common escalation we
  see from new agents.
- Forgetting that the approval must be in the case record. A message in chat is not an
  approval.
- Assuming a billing-defect refund still needs approval. It does not, if there is an open
  incident record.

## Slide 7 — Practice scenarios

A customer asks for 9,400 USD back on a duplicated annual subscription charge. You have
verified the duplicate. No manager approval is needed; action it and note the duplicate in
the case.

A customer asks for 12,000 USD back after cancelling a multi-seat plan mid-term. Manager
approval is needed before release.
