# Dublin Office Move — FAQ

## When is the move?

The Dublin team moves to the new floor over the weekend of the 14th. Desks are allocated by
team, and the seating plan is on the noticeboard by the kitchen.

## Do I need to pack?

Pack your personal items into the crate labelled with your name. Facilities will move
monitors and docking stations. Do not pack chairs.

## Will there be parking?

There are fewer spaces at the new floor. Parking is first come, first served, and the bike
racks have moved to the basement.

## Who do I ask?

Facilities, via the usual channel. Please do not raise a support ticket for office queries;
it goes to the customer queue.
