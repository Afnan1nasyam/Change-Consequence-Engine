# EMEA Support Team Notes

Unofficial. Maintained by whoever is on rota. Last touched by someone who has since moved
teams.

## Retention

For EU customers we keep personal data for 45 days after closure, then it goes. I think this
was agreed on a call with Privacy but I could not find it written down anywhere.

## Refunds

The refund approval line is 10,000 USD, same as everywhere else. If the customer is upset
just get the manager anyway, it saves a callback.

## Escalation ceiling

Anything above 50,000 USD goes to the Head of Support rather than a team manager. This is
not in the policy as far as I can tell but it is what we have always done.
